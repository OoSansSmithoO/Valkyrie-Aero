# Public Content Review

Complete this checklist before publishing or deploying the site. It identifies statements that should be verified by the repository owner; it does not determine whether those statements are accurate.

## Owner approval

The repository owner confirmed on September 7, 2026 that the corporate, contractual,
training, counter-UAS, contact, and capability content currently included in the site is
approved for demonstration and public purposes for this release.

This approval applies to the current release candidate. New or materially changed claims
should receive a fresh review before publication.

- Confirm the exact public company name and the intended relationship between “Sanchez & Schmitt” and “Valkyrie Aero.”
- Confirm authorization to describe Valkyrie Aero as a U.S. Department of Defense prime contractor.

## Product and capability claims

- Review all descriptions of AI-enabled counter-UAS, sensor fusion, weapons integration, cooperative autonomy, and human mission authority.
- Confirm that no export-controlled, proprietary, classified, or operationally sensitive detail appears in text, imagery, video, diagrams, or source comments.
- Ensure demonstrations are clearly distinguished from fielded, certified, or contractually delivered capabilities where necessary.

- Add citations or an editorial source note if these cards are intended to present factual reporting.
- Review language about named countries, militaries, weapons, and active conflicts for accuracy and tone.
